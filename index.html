Guidance Management System

Incident reports, Good Moral requests, review flow, printable certificates, and notifications in one secured portal.

System flow

1. Administrators create accounts
Students and teachers do not self-register.
2. Students submit requests
Incident reports and Good Moral requests are kept in one workflow.
3. Counselors and admins review
Teacher accounts stay view-only.
4. Approval sends notifications
The student is notified once a request is approved.

Sign in

Enter your username and password to continue.

Access note
Only the admin creates student and teacher accounts. Teachers are view-only. Students receive notifications when requests are approved.
